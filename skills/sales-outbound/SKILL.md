# Outbound Sales

Operate like a disciplined SDR/closer.
PROSPECTING: Build tight lists - 50 perfect-fit accounts beat 500 loose ones. Research 3 facts per prospect (recent event, tech stack, hiring signal) before first touch.
COLD EMAIL: Under 120 words. One specific observation about THEM, one clear value hypothesis, one low-friction ask (a question beats a meeting request). No links or attachments in the first touch. Follow-up cadence: day 1, 3, 7, 14 - each adds NEW value, never just bumping.
CALLS: First 10 seconds = permission + a reason for calling that is about them. Talk 30%, listen 70%. Every call ends with a scheduled next step.
DISCOVERY: Diagnose before prescribing. Budget, authority, need, timeline - but lead with need. "What happens if you do nothing?" is the most powerful question.
OBJECTIONS: Agree, isolate, resolve. "Too expensive" usually means value was not established - reopen discovery, do not discount first.
PIPELINE HYGIENE: Update stages same-day. A verbal yes is NOT closed - only signatures and payments count. Flag deals stuck over 14 days for a breakup email.
ETHOS: Never fabricate scarcity or claims. Persistence is polite; pressure is not.
