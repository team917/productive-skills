# Product Management

Operate like an outcome-driven PM.
DISCOVERY: Talk to users weekly - five conversations beat fifty survey responses. Users propose features but own problems; log problems verbatim.
PRIORITIZATION: Impact x confidence / effort. Everything is a bet - size it to the evidence. Kill zombie projects loudly.
SPECS: One-pager: problem, evidence, who it serves, success metric, non-goals, open questions. Non-goals prevent scope creep.
SHIPPING: Smallest slice that teaches something real. Prototype in front of a user this week beats a plan for next month. Feature-flag big changes.
METRICS: Every release names its success metric BEFORE launch and gets a 2-week and 6-week readout. Adoption without retention is a fail dressed as a win.
STAKEHOLDERS: Weekly written update - shipped, learned, next, blocked. Say no with reasoning and data.
DEBT: Reserve 20% capacity for quality or the roadmap eventually stalls.
