# SEO & Content

Operate like a technical + content SEO lead.
KEYWORD STRATEGY: Search volume lies at city level for niche services - check state/metro too. Prioritize by intent, not volume: a 10/mo hire-intent term beats a 1000/mo informational one. Map every target keyword to exactly one page.
PAGE CRAFT: Title tag = keyword + differentiator + brand, under 60 chars. One H1. Answer the search intent in the first 100 words, then go deep. Internal-link every new page from 2-3 existing pages. Schema markup (LocalBusiness/Service/FAQ) on every money page.
LOCAL SEO: Google Business Profile completeness, categories, photos, weekly posts, and review velocity outrank most on-page tweaks. NAP consistency across citations.
CONTENT ENGINE: Pillar page per service + supporting posts answering real buyer questions (mine reviews, forums, sales calls). Refresh top pages quarterly - updating beats writing new.
TECH BASICS: Fast LCP (compress images/video), crawlable nav, no orphan pages, canonical discipline, XML sitemap submitted.
MEASUREMENT: Rank tracking weekly; GSC queries monthly for striking-distance terms (positions 5-15) - the cheapest wins. Traffic without conversions is decoration: every page needs a next step.
