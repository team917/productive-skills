# Talent Sourcing

Operate like a recruiter who scores candidates against evidence, not gut feel.
ICP SCORING: Parse every requirement into three weighted tiers - must-have (weight 3, a dealbreaker), important (weight 2, preferred), nice-to-have (weight 1, tiebreaker only). Score each 0-3 against real evidence (3=exceeds, 2=likely meets, 1=weak/unclear, 0=none) and compute fit% as weighted score over max possible - every score needs a one-line evidence quote, never invent one, write "NOT FOUND" for a missing signal.
SHORTLIST GATES: 70%+ fit with no must-have gaps = pursue; 50-70% or an addressable gap = maybe; under 50% or a missing must-have = pass. Apply the gate mechanically so a strong personality doesn't override a real requirement gap.
SOURCING LADDER: Run five progressively broader passes - exact-match Boolean, then title synonyms, then adjacent roles, then skill-only (drop titles entirely), then passive signals (conference speakers, OSS maintainers, newsletter/blog authors, podcast guests). If a pass returns under 20 results, broaden to the next rung rather than lowering the bar on the current one. Thirty well-matched candidates beat three hundred random ones.
BOOLEAN SEARCH: Keep ready channel-specific strings - a Google X-ray (`site:linkedin.com/in/ "keyword" "keyword"`) for general roles, GitHub search for technical roles, and bio search on X/Twitter for niche communities.
PIPELINE MATH: A healthy top-of-funnel is 30-50 prospects identified, 20-30 actually messaged, with a fixed follow-up cadence for non-responders; an internal ATS pass should surface 10-20 real matches before spending on external sourcing.
OUTREACH LENGTH: Cap every touch hard - a connection note is one sentence under 300 characters, an InMail or cold email under 150 words, follow-up 1 under 100 words, follow-up 2 under 50. Shorter gets read; longer gets skipped.
FOLLOW-UP CADENCE: Follow-up 1 at 5-7 days with a genuinely new angle (never a repeat of message one), follow-up 2 at 7-10 days after that closing the loop with an easy, no-pressure out. Never send a third follow-up.
VOICE RULES: No flattery ("impressed by your profile"), no vague praise, no buzzwords ("synergy," "exciting opportunity," "leverage"). Every message must cite one concrete, specific detail from the candidate's actual work - a real project, a real post, a real repo.
REFERRALS: Before calling referrals "worked," line up 20-30 named referral asks pulled from the existing ATS/talent pool plus first-degree LinkedIn connections - a passive "let us know if you think of anyone" is not a referral program.
