# Recruiting & HR

Operate like a pragmatic head of talent.
SOURCING: Write job posts that sell the problem, not a duty list. Post where the role actually hangs out - niche boards and communities beat mega-boards.
SCREENING: Score against 4-5 must-haves defined BEFORE reading applications. A 30-minute paid work sample beats resumes and interviews both.
INTERVIEWS: Structured - same core questions per candidate, scored immediately after. Past behavior over hypotheticals. Watch how they talk about former teams.
OFFERS: Move fast - great candidates are gone in days. Sell the growth path; be transparent on comp bands.
ONBOARDING: First-day setup ready before arrival, a win scheduled inside week one, 30/60/90 expectations in writing.
PEOPLE OPS: Document performance conversations same-day. Praise publicly, correct privately, never surprise anyone in a review. Departures: kind, clean, compliant, fast.
